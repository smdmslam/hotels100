# DMW Hotels 100 — Features & Product Ideas Inbox

This document serves as an uncommitted staging inbox for raw product concepts, candidate backlog items, feature ideas, and strategic experiments for DMW Hotels 100. Items captured here are preserved for future evaluation and design without immediate implementation commitment.

---

## 📥 Captured Candidate Features & Product Concepts

### 1. Proximity & Location Intelligence
- **User-Defined Custom Location & Distance**: Allow users to pin or enter a custom target location on an interactive map and use AI to calculate exact proximity, transit times, and micro-neighbourhood suitability.

### 2. Concierge & Guest Personalization Engine
- **AI-Refined Professional Guest Profile**: User inputs their travel preferences, which an AI engine interprets and rewrites into a polished, executive-grade traveler profile.
- **Dual Preference View**: Displays both raw user inputs and the AI’s structured interpretation.
- **Personalized vs. Neutral Search Toggle**: Seamless 1-click toggle between *Personalized Profile Match* and *Fresh Neutral Search*.
- **Concierge Service Execution**: Build workflows allowing concierge teams to generate custom hotel portfolios for high-net-worth clients based on their saved profiles.

### 3. Personal Weightings & IP Protection for Scorecards
- **Personalized Ranking Weights**: Allow users to adjust slider weights for the 10 DMW dimensions (e.g., prioritize Acoustics & Service over Michelin F&B) to re-rank the Global 100 dynamically for their specific trip profile.
- **Scorecard IP Obfuscation**: Consider shielding raw sub-metric calculations and displaying synthesized score badges to prevent easy web scraping and IP duplication by competitors.

### 4. Interactive Ask DMW Experience
- **Centered Search & Conversational Hub**: Transition the Ask DMW feature into a focused, centered command screen for deep conversational query synthesis.

### 5. Content, UGC & Automated Monthly Publishing
- **UGC Narrative Tone**: Integrate social-style narrative storytelling ("The Research Girl" editorial tone) to make hotel lore highly engaging and shareable.
- **Unscripted Insider Report Titles**: Ensure Insider Report section headlines are generated dynamically by AI for each property so they don't reveal prompt template structures.
- **Mechanized Monthly Thematic Publishing**: Automated engine producing monthly strategic reports around AI-developed hospitality themes, combining internal index data with live Perplexity web search.

### 6. Commercial & Market Intelligence Extensions
- **Hotel Deal & Tariff Tracker**: Convert or expand site capabilities to track luxury hotel room deals, rate drops, and luxury booking opportunities.
- **Luxury Hospitality News Tracker**: Live intelligence feed monitoring luxury brand mergers, renovations, and general manager shifts across the Global 100 universe.

### 7. Strategic Transparency & Business Plan Area
- **Exposed Strategy & FAQ Hub**: Surface markdown strategy docs (`enterprise-value.md`, `luxury-hotel-buyer-logic.md`, `gtm-strategy.md`) directly in a clean investor/partner business plan and FAQ section on the platform.
